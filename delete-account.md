# Delete your Stella account

**Last updated: 09/10/2026**

You can delete your Stella account and all of its data at any time.

## Delete in the app

1. Open Stella.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account and all app data are deleted immediately.

## Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email **stella.northpath@gmail.com** from the email address or phone number linked to your account, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

Your login and all app data, including your tasks, check-in answers, focus sessions, Squirrel captures, and village progress.

## What may be kept

- Anonymous crash and usage statistics collected through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, which Google keeps under its own retention policy.
- Purchase records held by Google Play under Google's own policies.
- Limited information that may persist briefly in backups before being overwritten, or records we must keep by law.

## Subscriptions

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a Google Play subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it in the Play Store under **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.

For more details, see our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy).
