Delete your Stella account

Stella — a focus and productivity app by Northpath

You can ask us to delete your Stella account and the personal data associated with it at any time. We currently handle deletion requests by email, and we action them promptly.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to stella.northpath@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my account”.
  3. Send it from the email address linked to your account, or include the phone number you use to sign in to Stella, so we can verify the request is really yours.
We may ask one follow-up question to confirm your identity before deleting, so that no one else can delete your account on your behalf.

What gets deleted

When we process your request, we delete the personal data tied to your account, including:

  • Your account details (phone number / email used to sign in)
  • Your tasks and their step breakdowns
  • Your check-in answers and state history
  • Your focus session records and captures

Voice recordings are never stored — they are discarded after transcription — so there is nothing further to remove there.

What may be kept, and for how long

Some limited information may remain briefly in encrypted backups before it is overwritten in the normal backup cycle. We may also retain a minimal record where we are required to by law (for example, tax or accounting records relating to a subscription payment). Everything else is deleted.

We aim to complete deletion requests within a reasonable period and will confirm by email once it is done.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription, please also cancel it from Google Play → Subscriptions to avoid further billing.

Questions

For anything about your data or this process, contact our Grievance Officer, Sameer Mohammad, at stella.northpath@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your data.
